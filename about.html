Our Story

Designing Spaces
That Inspire

Mohalkar Architects & Planners was founded with a singular belief: that good architecture is the intersection of art, science, and human experience. Led by Ar. Abhishek Mohalkar, the studio has rapidly grown into a trusted name across Maharashtra and beyond.

We work across residential, commercial, interior, and urban scales — always beginning with careful listening and ending with spaces that outlast trends. From concept to construction documentation, we stay invested at every step.

Our philosophy is simple: Architecture should serve people first. Every decision — structural, spatial, material — is made with the end-user in mind.

Our Core Values

Vision

We see beyond the brief. Every project is an opportunity to imagine a better way of living, working, and inhabiting space.

Craft

Detail is not an afterthought — it is the thing. We obsess over every joint, proportion, and material choice.

Sustainability

We design with longevity in mind — buildings that age gracefully, consume less, and give back to their context.

People

Architecture is ultimately for people. We listen, collaborate, and centre every decision on the human experience.
